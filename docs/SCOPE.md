# Project Scope & Boundaries: Canon EOS 60D Interactive Experience

## 1. Project Vision
Recreate the interactive, cinematic product showcase for the Canon EOS 60D as demonstrated in the reference video. The experience features an immersive warm orange/amber canvas, oversized cyan display typography, compact floating dark glass HUD panels, a high-fidelity camera presentation with rotation, articulated screen movement, SD card ejection, and a multi-part exploded mechanical view synchronized to scroll progression.

## 2. In-Scope Deliverables
1. **Intro / Preloader Transition**: Expanding angular cone reveal with tilted Canon typography.
2. **Global Viewport Chrome**:
   - Top navigation bar: `eos` wordmark with "A LEGACY IN EVERY SHOT" brand lockup, pill navigation tabs (`18.0 MP SENSOR`, `FULL HD 1080P`, `CROSS-TYPE AF`, `VIEW SPECS`), and primary CTA pill.
   - Persistent bottom controls: Scroll indicator ("SCROLL TO DISCOVER") and dynamic chapter pagination ("01 / 07").
   - 3D drag-to-rotate interaction indicator pill.
3. **Scroll-Driven Storytelling Sequence (12 Canonical Scenes)**:
   - Scene 1: 18MP CMOS Sensor & Camera Overview.
   - Scene 2: Pentaprism macro zoom & Optical Heritage ("Canon" typography).
   - Scene 3: Top plate ergonomics, dials, and controls.
   - Scene 4: Contoured grip & two-stage shutter feel (with media preview strip).
   - Scene 5: Vari-angle 3.0" articulated LCD screen opening with live video texture & switcher pills (01, 02, 03).
   - Scene 6: EF/EF-S lens mount & optical perspective.
   - Scene 7: SD card door opening & memory card ejection animation.
   - Scene 8: 3D exploded view separating into 70+ floating precision components ("INSIDE").
   - Scene 9: APS-C CMOS sensor core isolation & micro-lens light gathering.
   - Scene 10: DIGIC 4 processing engine & burst performance.
   - Scene 11: Reverse convergence / reassembly back into unified camera body ("TOGETHER").
   - Scene 12: Monogram finale with camera centered inside giant "e o s" typography.
4. **Interactive Controls & Panels**:
   - Floating dark translucent glass HUD card with reactive copy, spec metrics, and pill buttons.
   - Active LCD video playback and channel switching.
   - Manual 3D orbit dragging when scroll motion settles.
   - Interactive specification overlay when "VIEW SPECS" / "TECH SPECS" is clicked.
5. **Responsive & Accessible Presentation**:
   - Desktop baseline (1440px) down to tablet and mobile viewports.
   - `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance.

## 3. Explicit Non-Goals (Out of Scope)
- No user authentication, user logins, or session storage.
- No database / backend persistence (Firestore, SQL, OAuth not required).
- No checkout, payment gateways, or real e-commerce transactions.
- No arbitrary AI chatbots, generic widgets, or unrequested product pages.
- No deviation from the reference visual hierarchy or color balance.
